# NEXORA Roadmap — Proposed

1. Draft whitepaper and open parameter decisions.
2. Protocol specification and deterministic test vectors.
3. Local prototype and automated tests.
4. Private multi-node testnet.
5. Public testnet, monitoring, bug reporting and documentation.
6. Independent security review and legal review.
7. Mainnet launch decision only after all critical issues are resolved.

This is a proposed sequence, not a promise of delivery dates or a claim that any stage is complete.
