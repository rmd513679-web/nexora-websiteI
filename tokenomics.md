# NEXORA Tokenomics — Working Draft

Status: experimental design draft; not a launched cryptocurrency or investment offer.

## Supply plan
- Maximum supply cap: **30,000,000 NXR**.
- Proposed founder allocation: **2,000,000 NXR (6.6667%)**.
- Intended lifetime mining-emission target: **approximately 20,000,000 NXR (66.6667%)**.
- Unissued ecosystem/reserve allocation: **8,000,000 NXR (26.6667%)**.

These are planned allocations only. The current prototype does not implement a founder genesis allocation, vesting/lock, reserve wallet, transfers, or a production ledger.

## Proposed mining schedule
- Initial reward: 50 NXR per block.
- Halving interval in this draft: 200,000 blocks.
- Rewards are represented in 1/100,000,000 NXR units and halve at each interval.
- A 200,000-block era with a 50 NXR initial reward approaches 20,000,000 NXR lifetime mining emission, subject to smallest-unit rounding. Add an emission-schedule test before freezing protocol parameters.
- 30,000,000 NXR is a hard ceiling, not a promise that all coins will be issued.

## Exchanges
Coins are not automatically placed on an exchange. Trading requires issued transferable coins, willing sellers, liquidity, and exchange acceptance. No listing, price, profit, or liquidity is promised.

## Before launch
Publish founder vesting/lock terms, reserve-release rules, allocation addresses, test vectors, and independent security-review results. Never request users' private keys or seed phrases.
